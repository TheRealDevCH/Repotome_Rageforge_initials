What it does

Six reflective faces, no frame.

Not a texture trick. The mod renders the scene a second time from the mirror's point of view, so reflections move, parallax and stack exactly like the real thing.

🧊

Full cube reflection

All six sides mirror independently. Walk around it and the reflection follows you from every angle — no fixed front.

👤

You stay invisible

World, mobs and blocks appear in the reflection; your own player model never does. A mirror that refuses to show you.

♾️

Recursive mirrors

Face two cubes at each other and the reflection re-enters itself, as deep as recursionDepth allows.

🎛️

Tunable to your rig

Seven values in one JSON file trade visual depth against framerate — from potato-safe to showcase quality.

🧱

Classic wall mirror too

Besides the cube you get the original flat, horizontally-facing mirror block for interiors and builds.

⚡

Sodium compatible

During a reflection pass the mod points Sodium's terrain projection at the mirror's oblique frustum, then restores it.

Read this first: the mod is performance-heavy.

Real reflections mean the game re-renders parts of the scene for every mirror face on screen, on top of everything it already draws. Depending on hardware and settings this can noticeably cut your FPS — especially with several cubes visible at once or a high recursion depth.

The good news: you are fully in control. Every performance-relevant value lives in config/mirror.json. Turn them down for smooth gameplay on a weaker PC, or push them up if your rig can take deeper, sharper reflections.

Configuration

Seven dials in config/mirror.json

Lower values mean better performance. Higher values mean richer reflections and more GPU/CPU load.

config/mirror.json 
{
  "maxReflections": 10,
  "recursionDepth": 4,
  "nestedPerParent": 2,
  "maxRenderPasses": 48,
  "reflectionDistanceChunks": 10,
  "fogStartBlocks": 100,
  "resolutionScale": 0.85
}
resolutionScaleBiggest FPS win

Render resolution of the reflection relative to your normal resolution. 1.0 is full quality.

Drop to 0.6–0.7 first if you need frames back.
recursionDepthHigh impact

How many times a mirror may reflect into another mirror. Higher gets closer to a true infinite mirror.

Lower it if two facing cubes tank your framerate.
maxReflectionsMedium impact

Maximum number of mirror surfaces rendered at the same time.

Lower it when many mirrors are visible at once.
maxRenderPassesSafety net

Hard cap on total render passes spent on reflections — your overall performance budget.

Use it as a strict FPS guardrail regardless of other values.
nestedPerParentMedium impact

How many nested reflections are allowed per parent reflection.

Lower it if complex mirror setups feel laggy.
reflectionDistanceChunksSituational

How far, in chunks, the reflected view reaches into the world.

Lower it if distant terrain in reflections doesn't matter to you.
fogStartBlocksSituational

Distance at which fog fades the reflection out, hiding far-away render cost.

Lower it to fade reflections sooner and save work.
Quick tuning recipe: if the mod feels heavy, cut resolutionScale to 0.65 and recursionDepth to 2 first. That combination usually recovers the most frames for the smallest visual trade-off. Full reasoning in the performance model.
Setup

Installed in five steps.

Standard Fabric workflow — no launcher plugins, no extra tooling.

Install Fabric Loader 0.19+For Minecraft Java Edition 26.2 on Java 25+.
Add Fabric APIDownload the 26.2 build and drop it into your mods folder.
Copy both mod jarsPut mirror-…jar and rageforge-…jar into mods. Both are required.
Optional: pre-tune performanceCopy config/mirror.json into your config folder before first launch.
Launch and open the Rageforge tabFind the Mirror Cube and the classic Mirror in the creative inventory.
Compatibility

Works with Sodium. Not with Iris.

✓ Sodium

Supported. During a reflection pass the mod points Sodium's terrain projection at the mirror's oblique projection, so Sodium draws the reflected view instead of the scene behind the mirror — then restores it.

✕ Iris / Shaders

Declared as breaks in the mod metadata. Iris replaces the render pipeline that the reflection pass re-enters, so mirrors cannot draw their view.
